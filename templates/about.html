About MoltMeter

AI-powered molt detection for sustainable green crab harvesting

Our Mission

Enable sustainable green crab harvesting through AI-powered molt detection.

MoltMeter helps fisheries workers identify green crabs at optimal harvest timing by predicting molt phase from images. Our technology supports the development of a sustainable soft-shell crab industry while managing invasive green crab populations along the New Hampshire and Maine coastline.

How It Works

  • Capture: Upload or stream live video of green crabs
  • Analyze: AI analyzes visual indicators to predict days until molt
  • Visualize: Color-coded overlays show molt phase categories
  • Harvest: Identify "peeler" crabs ready for immediate harvest
Molt Phase Categories
Peeler (<1 day) Harvest immediately!
Imminent (<3 days) Harvest now
Pre-molt Near (<5 days) Monitor closely
Pre-molt Early (<14 days) Check again soon
Inter-molt (>14 days) Not close to molting

Use Cases

Commercial Fisheries

Optimize harvest timing for soft-shell crab operations. Maximize yield by identifying crabs in the critical peeler window.

Marine Research

Study molt cycles and green crab biology. Track population molt patterns and validate biological models.

Invasive Species Management

Support sustainable utilization of invasive green crab populations. Turn an ecological problem into an economic opportunity.

Technology

MoltMeter leverages computer vision for ecology technologies, trained on hundreds of green crab molt cycles to provide accurate predictions in field conditions.

The system combines object detection to identify crabs in images with advanced machine learning models that analyze visual molt indicators such as color, texture, and carapace condition.
